About the Project and Dataset

The IntegraCAR project aims to gather information to support the regularization of Rural Environmental Registries (CAR). Our dataset brings together satellite images and labeled maps to identify different types of land use and land cover in Espírito Santo in 2020. The data serves as a basis for research and training of AI models applied to geoprocessing.

The IntegraCAR-LC10K is developed by the Federal Institute of Espírito Santo (Ifes) in partnership with Idaf, Fapes, and Seger, with funding via the Inova Seger 2025 Notice. SRS: EPSG:31984.

Technical Specifications

Source Satellites

KOMPSAT-3/3A Constellation (RGB Bands).

Spatial Resolution

Very high resolution of 0.5 m/pixel (50 cm per pixel).

Data Format

Image/Segmentation Mask pairs in 2048×2048 pixels.

Projection/SRS

SRS EPSG:31984.

Sampling Methodology

Geographic stratification with 1,000 sampled coordinates for each of the 10 official microregions of Espírito Santo (SEDES/IDAF), ensuring territorial diversity and representativeness.

Automated Acquisition Workflow via API

Custom Data Collection API

To enable the creation of the dataset and ensure reproducibility, we developed a custom API that automates the querying of the GeoBases platform's Web Map Service (WMS). This API performs the automated download of orthophotomosaics and thematic maps from geographic coordinates, replacing slow manual processes in QGIS. This enables a simplified and standardized geographic expansion to other regions.

View API Repository on GitHub
  • Sampling

    Selection of 10,000 stratified coordinates from CAR properties.

  • Query

    The API automatically accesses the GeoBases portal via WMS services.

  • Download

    Extraction of satellite image crops and maps at 2048 x 2048 pixels size.

  • Processing

    Conversion of original thematic maps (shapefiles) into segmentation masks.

  • Result

    Generation of 10,000 ready pairs (image and mask), replacing manual work in QGIS.

Nomenclature & Taxonomy

Grouped and optimized semantic taxonomy for the Rural Environmental Registry (CAR), developed in partnership with IDAF.

Vegetation Areas

Original Grouped Classes: Native forests, restinga, mangroves, marshes/swamps, and altitude grasslands.

Description: Consolidated natural arboreal or shrub vegetation and protected wetlands.

Agropastoral Areas

Original Grouped Classes: Pastures, cultivated crops, silviculture, exposed soil, and initial regeneration areas aimed at productive use.

Description: Lands managed for agricultural activities, active pasture, or commercial reforestation.

Infrastructure

Original Grouped Classes: Buildings, paved roads, dirt roads, sidewalks, mining/extraction, and other human-built surfaces.

Description: Physical structures and anthropogenic transportation routes.

Water Bodies

Original Grouped Classes: Rivers, lakes, reservoirs, canals, and other visible water surfaces.

Description: Natural or artificial surface water resources.

Macega

Original Grouped Classes: Macega (kept as an independent class).

Description: Pioneer, rustic, neglected, or transitional vegetation, composed of tall grasses and tangled shrubs, generally associated with fallow areas or abandoned pastures.

Model Repository & Source Code

Open Source Training Code & Weights

To complement the IntegraCAR-LULC dataset, we provide the complete source code used to train and evaluate our baseline semantic segmentation models. The repository includes implementations of U-Net and DeepLabv3 architectures, data loading pipelines, training scripts, and pre-trained weights. This allows researchers to easily reproduce our benchmarks or fine-tune the models for specific environmental applications.

Official Scientific Paper

IntegraCAR-LULC-10K: A High-Resolution Optical Satellite Dataset for LULC Segmentation in the Brazilian Rural Environmental Registry

Authors: Calebe Albertino, Gabriel M. B. Lima, Vinicius R. Oliveira, Arthur R. V. Ribeiro, Eliza K. S. Oliveira, Eduardo H. P. Souza, Otavio G. Dalvi, Karin S. Komati, Jefferson O. Andrade, Francisco A. Boldt, Thiago M. Paixao

Affiliation: Instituto Federal do Espírito Santo (IFES) & Instituto de Defesa Agropecuária e Florestal do Espírito Santo (IDAF)

Land-use and land-cover (LULC) mapping in the context of the Brazilian Rural Environmental Registry (CAR) is essential for environmental governance, yet lacks publicly available machine learning benchmarks tailored to this domain. This paper introduces IntegraCAR-LULC-10K, a high-resolution remote sensing dataset for semantic segmentation of CAR-related areas in Espírito Santo, Brazil, built from KOMPSAT-3/3A orthophotomosaic imagery and thematic annotations from GeoBases (2019–2020)...

Dataset Limitations and Academic Citation

The reference annotations in this dataset were generated by experts from the Jones dos Santos Neves Institute (IJSN) through visual photointerpretation of optical satellite imagery (2019–2020). This process may introduce inherent uncertainties at class transition boundaries or in shadowed areas. Researchers should consider that the benchmark reflects the land cover conditions of that specific period and not necessarily the real-time state of the ground.
